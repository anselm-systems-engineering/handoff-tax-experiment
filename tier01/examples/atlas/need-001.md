Project Atlas must traverse loose, wind-compacted sand (dune field sectors 4–7)
without ground assistance, in the temperature envelope specified by the
stakeholder interview of 2026-09-08. The capability is expressed at mission
level: reach and survey, not climb performance.

Open question for the propulsion trade study: whether continuous-track,
wheel-leg hybrid, or air-cushion concepts remain viable after the mass
constraint (constraint-001) is applied.
