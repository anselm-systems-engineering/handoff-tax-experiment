Total vehicle mass, including energy storage and payload mounting points, must
not exceed 350 kg. Source: mechanical team memo M-114 (2026-09-10), which the
propulsion trade study must treat as binding. The constraint interacts with
need-001: sand capability historically scales with ground pressure, and light
vehicles pay for flotation in other subsystems.
