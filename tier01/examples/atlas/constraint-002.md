---
id: constraint-002
title: Failsafe limp-home on single-point sensor failure
type: constraint
status: active
author: safety-team
date: 2026-09-20
tags: [safety-critical, sensors]
---

On any single-point sensor failure in the propulsion loop, the system must
enter a failsafe limp-home mode rather than degrading to uncontrolled
behaviour. Raised by the safety team on 2026-09-19; applies to every sensor in
the propulsion chain, including motor controller telemetry.
